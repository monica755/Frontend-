CURRICULUM STRUCTURE

Stages of Learning

PRIMARY WING

Primary School (Grades 1 - 5)

Building strong foundations in numeracy, languages, environmental science, and social interaction through play and activity.

  • Interactive Phonics & Reading
  • Basic Math & Logic Building
  • Art, Music & Moral Stories
MIDDLE WING

Middle School (Grades 6 - 8)

Transitioning to deeper conceptual learning, experimental science, analytical problem-solving, and digital literacy.

  • Science & Computer Labs
  • Second & Third Languages
  • Project-Based Assignments
SECONDARY WING

Secondary School (Grades 9 - 10)

Comprehensive board exam preparation with focus on critical thinking, rigorous assessment, and self-discipline.

  • Intensive STEM Focus
  • Regular Remedial Coaching
  • Career Orientation Seminars
SENIOR SECONDARY

Higher Secondary (11 & 12)

Specialized subject streams preparing students for competitive entrance examinations and university education.

  • Science (Maths / Biology / CS)
  • Commerce & Accountancy
  • Competitive Exam Guidance
