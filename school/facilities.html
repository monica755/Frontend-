What We Offer

Smart Classrooms

Well-ventilated, multimedia-enabled classrooms equipped with digital projectors to make learning visual and engaging.

Modern Labs

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and high-speed Computer Laboratories for hands-on experimentation.

Library & Reading Room

Vast repository of academic books, reference encyclopedias, journals, periodicals, and value-based literature.

Sports & Play Area

Spacious playground for athletics, cricket, badminton, yoga sessions, and physical education drills.

Auditorium & Activity Hall

Dedicated spaces for cultural events, Gita chanting competitions, annual days, and youth assemblies.

Safety & Transport

24/7 CCTV surveillance, fire safety systems, pure RO drinking water, and safe school bus transportation.
