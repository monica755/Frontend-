About Our School

Discover our rich heritage, foundational principles, and commitment to value-based education.

OUR ROOTS

Madras Chinmaya Seva Trust

Chinmaya Vidyalaya, Nagapattinam, was established under the visionary guidance and blessing of Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayananda and the Madras Chinmaya Seva Trust.

We strive to offer students a harmonious blend of traditional cultural wisdom and progressive modern education. Our objective is not merely bookish knowledge, but character building, clarity of intellect, and social sensitivity.

GUIDING LIGHT

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To prepare students for life by fostering academic brilliance, moral integrity, cultural pride, and universal human values, enabling them to lead noble and productive lives.

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, inclusive learning environment that inspires curiosity, critical thinking, disciplined character, and selfless service to society and the nation.

Chinmaya Vision Program

Our holistic educational philosophy integrates 4 pillars: Integrated Personality Development, Indian Culture, Patriotism, and Universal Outlook.

Chinmaya Vidyalaya

Run under the auspices of Madras Chinmaya Seva Trust, empowering students with knowledge and character.
