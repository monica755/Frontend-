Box 1

Belle was a kind and brave girl. One day, her father was caught by a scary Beast in a big castle. To save him, Belle decided to stay in the castle.


Box 2

At first, she was afraid of the Beast. But soon, she saw that the Beast was not bad, he was just lonely. The Beast was kind and gentle. They became friends.


Box 3

One day, Belle heard that her father was sick. She asked the Beast if she could leave. He let her go because he cared about her happiness.


Box 4

When Belle returned, she found the Beast sick and weak. She held his hand and said she loved him. At that moment, the Beast turned into a handsome prince!


Box 5

The spell was broken because Belle loved him for his kind heart, not his looks. We learn that kindness and love are more important than how someone looks. True beauty is in the heart!
