EGS PILLAY ARTS AND SCIENCE COLLEGE

EGS Pillay Arts and Science College was established at Nagapattinam in 1996. EGS Pillay Arts and Science College strives to be the lead college among the degree colleges in terms of providing education, excellence and enlighten its students while continuing to meet its social commitments. EGS Pillay Arts and Science College provides programmes at a level of undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral to students. The EGS Pillay Arts and Science College provides BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BCA, M.Com, MBA, M.Sc, etc. Students are required to clear the eligibility criteria to get admission into EGS Pillay Arts and Science College. The courses offered at EGS Pillay Arts and Science College are affiliated with Bharathidasan University and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). Placement cell EGS Pillay Arts and Science College provides job opportunities to its students across the country with good pay packages. EGS Pillay Arts and Science College also facilitates hostels for boys and girls, library, sports, medical, auditorium and many more to the eligible students.
