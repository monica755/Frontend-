The Golden Egg: Patience and Contentment A farmer owned a special goose that laid a golden egg every single day. This brought the farmer and his wife steady wealth. However, they grew impatient and greedy. "Why wait for one egg a day," they thought, "when we could get all the gold at once?" So, they made a terrible mistake. The farmer killed the goose, believing he would find a treasure trove of gold inside. But when he cut the goose open, he found nothing special - just ordinary goose insides. By being greedy and impatient, they lost their consistent source of income and ended up with nothing. Moral: “Patience and gratitude are more important than greed.
