Police Department

The Nagapattinam Police Department is committed to maintaining law and order, protecting citizens, preventing crime, and ensuring public safety throughout the district. The department provides efficient policing, emergency response, traffic management, and public assistance services.

Nagapattinam Police Department – Contact Details

The Nagapattinam Police Department is headed by the Superintendent of Police (SP), Nagapattinam, who is responsible for maintaining law and order, preventing crime, ensuring traffic safety, and protecting the public across the district. The SP Office is the main administrative office where citizens can report complaints, seek police assistance, and obtain information about law enforcement services.

For general enquiries and official communication, citizens can contact the office at 04365-242888 or the official mobile number 94431 47711. In emergencies requiring immediate police assistance, dial 112, and for the Police Control Room, dial 100. The department operates 24×7, providing emergency response, crime prevention, traffic management, women's safety, cybercrime assistance, and community policing throughout Nagapattinam District, Tamil Nadu.
