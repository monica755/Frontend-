Education Department

The Nagapattinam Education Department works under the Tamil Nadu School Education Department to provide quality education and improve learning standards across the district. The department is headed by the Chief Educational Officer (CEO) and supervises government and government-aided schools. It is responsible for school administration, teacher training, public examinations, scholarships, student welfare programs, and the implementation of government education schemes.
