Company Name:Wipro

About

Wipro Limited is a global, AI-powered information technology, consulting, and business process services company headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Operating in 65 countries with over 240,000 employees, it provides cloud computing, cybersecurity, data analytics, and digital transformation solutions to enterprise clients worldwide.

Services

Wipro Technology Services: IT consulting, enterprise application implementation (like SAP and Oracle), cloud management, data and analytics, and artificial intelligence-driven solutions.Wipro Consulting Services: Strategic business advisory focused on digital transformation, organizational change, and process optimization.Wipro Engineering Edge: Research and development services covering embedded systems, semiconductors, software-defined systems, and Industry 4.0.Wipro Business Process Services (BPS): Outsourcing and business process management (BPM), including automated workflows and next-generation customer service.
